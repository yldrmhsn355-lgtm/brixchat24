# Data retention and privacy

Retention must be configured per contract and jurisdiction. Default operational guidance: application logs 30 days, worker heartbeats 7 days, security audit 365 days, message/media content per tenant contract, backups per the backup policy. Privacy export/delete/restrict requests are authenticated, tenant-scoped and audited. Legal hold blocks deletion until counsel releases it. A scheduled production retention job and counsel-approved periods are required before launch.
